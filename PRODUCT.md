# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Small-to-medium businesses (SMBs), local organizations, and underserved clients looking for high-performance web development, SEO digital visibility, and proactive cybersecurity audits without enterprise bloat or template lag.

## Product Purpose

NZI Conduit empowers businesses with dedicated IT engineering solutions. The site serves as a high-converting marketing surface, building immediate technical trust, showcasing Core Web Vitals performance benchmarks, and driving direct technical audit requests.

## Brand Personality

Engineering Excellence, Trustworthy, Modern & High-Performance. Professional yet approachable, transparent, and technically rigorous.

## Anti-references

Generic template bloat, overly saturated AI-scaffolded designs, cramped typography, cluttered glassmorphism overlays, and gimmicky non-standard interactions.

## Design Principles

1. **Craft Above Templates**: Every visual component must feel handcrafted, tailored, and senior-engineer-designed rather than AI-generated or template-derived.
2. **Speed & Clarity**: Prioritize lightning-fast interactions, zero layout shift (CLS 0), crisp typography contrast, and transparent SLA response guarantees.
3. **Intentional Rhythm & Hierarchy**: Every section has a single dominant focal point, using layered neutral surfaces to create horizontal visual rhythm without adding clutter.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliant contrast (body text ≥4.5:1, headings ≥3:1), keyboard-navigable focus traps, screen-reader semantic landmarks, and full support for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
